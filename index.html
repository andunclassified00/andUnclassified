Statement

We started and unclassified because the artists we admired kept getting asked which box they belonged in. Painter, or maker? Designer, or artist? We think the question is the wrong size for the work.

Instead of a portfolio, each artist gets a case file — a record of process, material, and the questions the work is actually asking. Nothing here is sorted by medium. It's sorted by who made it.
